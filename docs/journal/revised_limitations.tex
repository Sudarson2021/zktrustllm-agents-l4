\section{Limitations and Required Next Milestones}

The current artifact is a strong local research prototype but not yet a final production or public-chain deployment. First, the full Hardhat suite must remain green after removing or updating stale legacy tests. Second, the current ablation runner emits reviewable manifests, while full end-to-end ablation reruns must still be executed for final statistical claims. Third, public testnet validation is not reported until a funded testnet key and RPC endpoint are available. Fourth, the AuthV2.2 verifier exists and deploys locally, but positive and negative frozen AuthV2.2 proof payloads remain to be generated. Fifth, RBAC security claims are scoped to the publication-target AuthV2.2 attestor; older attestor variants should either be explicitly treated as legacy artifacts or upgraded for consistency. These limitations are concrete engineering milestones rather than conceptual gaps in the proposed architecture.
