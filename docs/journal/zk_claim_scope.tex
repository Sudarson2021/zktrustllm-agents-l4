\subsection{Scope of the ZK Claim}

The implementation contains Groth16/BN254 verifier lineage for AuthV1, AuthV2.1, and AuthV2.2. A frozen AuthV2.1 proof payload is available, and the AuthV2.2 verifier deployment path is exercised locally. However, the current manuscript does not claim a frozen AuthV2.2 proof artifact until positive and negative AuthV2.2 proof payloads are generated and checked into the reproducibility package. The ZK contribution is therefore stated as Groth16-compatible bounded authorization support, not as a completed production ZK deployment.
